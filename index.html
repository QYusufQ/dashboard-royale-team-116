One Payout Lead: M-1158

Kept 3% instead of 10% (luck z −4.6). A lead to inspect, not proof of a fault.

Evidence

Location: Back Forty play per working hour is 36% below the floor average, almost all from empty seats (occupancy −35%, bet size −1%).

Game: Comet Cash and Lucky Lanterns have above-average occupancy but a $1 max bet. They are low-stakes games, not unpopular ones.

Machine: only 5 machines are weaker than their own bank and game in both halves, by 6–14%. Machine effects barely repeat between Jan–Mar and Apr–Jun (correlation 0.29).

Payouts: the floor kept 9.50% of coin-in against a designed 9.46%, so there is no floor-wide leak. M-1158 is the one exception.

Replacements: the two real swaps barely helped. The new Royal Draw added only $0.39 theo per hour.

Decision

Don't replace the bottom 10. Leasing replacements costs $16,900 and recovers at most about $3,250 in July.

Inspect M-1158 for a payout fault ($1,200).

Move Back Forty machines into the 2 empty Center Pit slots, where play runs about 85% higher.

How Concentrated Are Losses?

Key Finding

A few players carry most of the losses.

  • Top 10% of losing players = 53% of all losses

Players vs Losses, by Tier

Responsible Play

Gold and Platinum are about 6% of players but 57% of losses. Tiers are earned by playing, so this is association, not cause.

We suggest only protective ideas for heavy losers, never actions that target them to gamble more.

Cheaper comps didn't bring more play.

  • May 1: comps cost ~53% fewer points in every tier

Risk to Flag

Each unredeemed point is now worth about twice as much in comps, so the future cost of saved points has roughly doubled.

July 2025 Forecast

Floor total with its P10–P90 band, split by zone. The dashed grey line is the no-promotion P90: the promotion counts as working only if Jul 18–20 coin-in beats about $4.81M.

Heat Explains Part of June

Each °C above 30°C lowers coin-in by 0.24%, about −2% at June's average high.

Forecast Misses Come from Foot Traffic

On days the forecast was off, it was mostly because more or fewer people came in than usual. Giving the model that day's actual foot traffic cuts June error by 37%, but traffic is only known after the day, so it explains misses rather than predicting them.

Limitations

The heat effect only appears once June is included, so it is an assumption for July rather than a validated feature. July is set at its normal high of 104.5°F (40.3°C), from NOAA's 1991–2020 normals for Las Vegas (NWS Las Vegas). January–June daily highs are real NOAA observations from Las Vegas airport, supplied with the datathon data. July 4 adds extra uncertainty. A next-day model with lagged sales reaches $38.3K, but it can only forecast one day ahead, so it isn't used for the July plan.

Q6 · The $100,000 plan

♥ Recommendations

Fund 3 actions for $19,800 in July and keep $80,200.

Inspect M-1158

Cost: $1,200 (1-day lead)

Expected July benefit: $0 – $2.9K – $5.8K. About $73K a year if a fault is fixed.

Uncertainty: High. Assumes a 50% chance of a fixable payout fault.

Measurement: Inspection finding; July actual hold vs the designed 10%.

Move 2 Back Forty Machines to Center Pit

Cost: $3,600 (2-day lead)

Expected July benefit: $6.7K – $13.5K – $16.2K

Uncertainty: Medium. M-1152's move doubled its play, but there are only two empty slots.

Measurement: Coin-in per working hour vs the machines' old level and their new neighbours.

Promotion Weekend, Jul 18–20

Cost: $15,000 (14-day lead)

Expected July benefit: $34.6K – $60.6K – $86.5K

Uncertainty: Medium. Assumes summer gets 40–100% of the Winter Tournament's $86.5K net lift.

Measurement: Jul 18–20 coin-in above $4.81M, the forecast's no-promotion P90; theo, comps and F&B vs normal same weekdays.

How Sure Are We? 10,000 Simulated Julys

Our plan pays off even in its worst case.

  • Every action at its low case at once still nets +$21.6K
  • The manager's plan loses about $33.7K: replacements gain almost nothing
