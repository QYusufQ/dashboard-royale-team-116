The Two Humps

Low-performing machines cluster primarily in Back Forty and a handful of game themes.

Evidence

Location effect: Machines in the Back Forty zone suffer from 35% lower occupancy than average floor space, driving down overall coin-in regardless of machine theme. Low-stakes design: Games like Comet Cash and Lucky Lanterns have normal occupancy rates, but lower average bet caps ($1 max) that reduce total dollar play. Noise vs. underperformance: Only a tiny fraction of machine-level variance comes from the machine itself. Most "underperformance" is driven by placement, room traffic, and bet limits rather than a bad game cabinet.

Decision

Reallocate low-earning themes: Move high-stakes games into underperforming zones like Back Forty to boost dollar yield per active seat. Retrain layout around traffic: Shift low-stakes/high-occupancy games into high-foot-traffic areas to maximize table and machine utilization. Avoid hasty replacements: Hold off on spending budget to buy 10 replacement machines immediately, as game replacements (like Royal Draw) only yield minor return improvements that do not pay back full machine replacement costs.
